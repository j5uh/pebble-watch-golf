Golf GPS yardages on your wrist, for Pebble Round 2 and Pebble Time 2.

- Front, middle and back of the green, plus a layup target that leaves your preferred distance.
- Reach and carry yardages for the bunkers and water still ahead of you.
- A greenside view of the green and its bunkers, and a hole map with your position.
- Pick a course from a nearest-first list. Courses can be saved on your phone for play without signal.
- Up/Down changes holes; Select cycles views; hold Select for settings. Yards or meters.

Distances use your phone's GPS (keep the Pebble app running). Stale or missing fixes are shown as unavailable, never guessed. Green targets are the mapped green, not today's pin.

Course maps © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Open source (MIT): https://github.com/j5uh/pebble-watch-golf
Missing your course? Request it from the app's phone settings page.
